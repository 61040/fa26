## Lower Charles, spring 2026

The survey records water depth, flow, and visible changes to the riverbank at
the same twelve locations each week.

The work is deliberately low-tech: a measuring staff, a notebook, and repeatable
observations. The resulting record helps identify changes that are easy to miss
from one day to the next.
