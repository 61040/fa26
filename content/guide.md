## Course Overview

This is a 15-unit junior/senior design class covering software design principles, web development, and AI integration. The course emphasizes designing software that meets user needs while being flexible and easy to use.

## Key Requirements

**Prerequisites**: 6.1020 (Software Construction) and 6.1200 (Mathematics for Computer Science)

**Lectures**: Two 80-minute sessions weekly (Mon/Wed, 2:35-3:55pm, Room 1-190)

**Recitations**: Optional 50-minute weekly sessions led by TAs

**Attendance**: Lecture attendance is required; recitations are optional

## Grading Breakdown

- Personal project: 45%
- Team project: 30%
- Problem sets: 20%
- Preps: 5%

## Late Submission Policy

- Within one week late: 20% penalty
- More than one week late: No credit
- Students receive three "slacks" allowing 3-day extensions without penalty (not applicable to team projects)

## Collaboration & Academic Integrity

Students may collaborate and discuss ideas freely, use third-party code with proper citation, and employ LLMs (with disclosure). However, individual written work must be completed personally. The class operates on an honor code basis.

## Support Resources

- Office hours for technical and design questions
- Class forum for public inquiries
- Private Piazza messages for personal/grading issues
- Individual lecturer meetings by appointment
