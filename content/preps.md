## About Preps

Preps are preparatory exercises completed before recitations, due at 10AM on recitation days. They're graded on effort rather than perfection—a plausible submission that indicates you made a good faith attempt to complete the assigned tasks will receive full credit.

Attend recitations to discuss any difficulties with the prep work.
