## About Preps

Preps are preparatory exercises completed before recitations, due at 10AM on recitation days. They're graded on effort rather than perfection---a plausible submission that indicates you made a good faith attempt to complete the assigned tasks will receive full credit. We are more interested in your learning than we are in punishing lateness; as such, preps submitted late do not cut into slack days.

Attend recitations to discuss any difficulties with the prep work.
