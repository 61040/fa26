---
title: Start with one branch
date: 2026-08-01
description: Notes on testing a structure before committing more material.
---

The best place to begin a large structure is not with a large structure. It is
with one branch placed where the current is easiest to understand.

<!--more-->

A small start reveals the pressure, the weak bank, and the material close at
hand. Once those facts are visible, each next choice becomes less dramatic.
Software projects are not so different.

Build the narrowest useful path. Watch what it teaches you. Then add the next
branch.
