---
title: Measuring the current
date: 2026-07-18
description: A field note on observing a river before changing it.
---

Fast water makes every intervention feel urgent. That is precisely when it pays
to pause and watch.

<!--more-->

Where does the current pull hardest? Which parts of the bank already hold? A few
careful observations often replace a much larger pile of hurried work.

Before changing a system, learn how it moves when nobody is touching it.
