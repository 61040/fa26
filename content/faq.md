## Registration & Access

**Can I register?** Registration isn't expected to be limited. Cross-registered students from other institutions are welcome.

**Can I listen/audit?** Listeners may attend and access materials, though they cannot receive feedback or join final project teams.

## Attendance Requirements

The course mandates in-person attendance at both weekly lectures. Students are expected to attend in person because the class emphasizes design concepts developed specifically for this course format.

## Prerequisites

While not strictly enforced, students lacking strong JavaScript skills and familiarity with concepts like data abstraction and immutability may struggle significantly.

## Key Changes for 2026

- Reduced from 18 to 15 units
- Mastery-focused grading
- Greater emphasis on identifying authentic demand and design value
- Systematic LLM integration (rather than informal "vibe coding")
- Students encouraged to choose their own project problems

## LLM Usage

The course teaches structured approaches to leverage language models for reducing boilerplate work while maintaining codebase control and understanding.
