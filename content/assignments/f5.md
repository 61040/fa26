Your beta checkpoint should be a functionally complete version of your app, and it should be deployed. Every concept in your design should be implemented, and each of your user journeys should work from start to finish in the deployed app.

You will run your user tests for F6 on this version, so it should be ready for people outside your team. This includes authentication, proper visual and interaction design, and testing your app yourself to ensure that your user testing reveals valuable insights (and is not spent pointing out simple/obvious bugs).

## Deliverables
- **Source code**: the current state of your app, in your team repo.
- **Tests**: your concept tests, with instructions for running them in the README of your team repo.
- **Updated designs**: your designs from [F3](./f3.md), updated to match what you built.
- **Updated development plan**: a new version of the plan explaining what progress you've made since [F4](./f4.md), what remains to be done before F7, and how your plans might have changed.
- **Deployed app**: a public URL pointing to your running app, running the commit you submit.
- **Screen recording**: a video showing the current state of your app, with an audio narration explaining some key features. Link it in the README of your team repo.

## Submission
Submit your assignment in Commons.
- Full link to the specific commit of your project repo
- The URL of your deployed app
- The link to your screen recording
