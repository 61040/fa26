Each of you will be assigned individually the task of critiquing another team project. For this milestone, you will critique that team's problem framing. Your critique is due **three days** after the deadline for the artifact itself.

You should structure your critique in three parts:
- **I like**. Some aspects of the artifact that you like, and why.
- **I wish**. Some aspects of the artifact that you wish were different in some way.
- **I wonder**. Some aspects you are wondering about; perhaps some suggestions for a simplification, a different approach, or whatever.

You should expect to each spend a few hours in total across all three critiques, and to write no more than half a page in total for this critique. In grading this, we will consider primarily how *helpful* your critique is likely to be the receiving team.

**Expectations.**
- Go to your assigned project team's thread and reply to the thread with the designated artifact.
Please reply directly to that message. Don't nest replies. Don't create higher level threads. See [here] for an example of where your critique should go.
