Your first meeting with your TA mentor is a chance to introduce your team, talk through your project idea, and agree on how you'll work together for the rest of the project. Arrange a time with your mentor and hold the meeting by the deadline. Every team member is expected to attend. If for some reason you are unable to come, you should let your mentor know in advance.

## Instructions

0. **Identify your TA mentor.** Regard the [table on Commons](https://class.mit-sdg.dev/t/6fe0c1f7-6ea5-4fe9-8bf4-c7a42939d8e3).
1. **Make a thread on Commons.** Make a new discussion thread on Commons.
   Manually add your entire time and your TA mentor by clicking "Choose people
   or groups..." and selecting each person individually. Publish the thread and
   notify the participants.
2. **Schedule a time to meet.** In the thread, plan a time to meet. The time should work for everyone on your team
   and the TA. **Class time on November 9th** is a good time to meet since
   there is no lecture that day.
3. **Attend the meeting.** The meeting will last no more than 40 minutes.

## "Deliverables"

It is _your_ job to structure the meeting and make sure that it is helpful to you; you should think of your mentor as a consultant rather than a grader.

- **Agenda**: topics to be discussed with time allocated to each
- **Project summary**: the bad situation you've identified and your solution ideas so far
- **Issues**: problems and issues to address, with a sentence or two explaining each
- **Plans & decisions** (completed after): plans for next week and decisions made

Create a separate folder in your team repo for your mentor meetings, and keep these deliverables there. You might, for example, have a single markdown file for each meeting with sections for each area. There is **no official submission**, but the quality of these deliverables will be taken into account at the end in determining a portion of your team's grade.

After this meeting, you'll continue with [weekly project updates](./final-project.md#project-updates).
