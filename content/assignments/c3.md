Find your critique assignment on Commons in the [final project assignments thread](https://class.mit-sdg.dev/t/6fe0c1f7-6ea5-4fe9-8bf4-c7a42939d8e3).
For this milestone, you will critique that team's problem framing.

You should structure your critique in three parts:

- **I like**. Some aspects of the artifact that you like, and why.
- **I wish**. Some aspects of the artifact that you wish were different in some way.
- **I wonder**. Some aspects you are wondering about; perhaps some suggestions for a simplification, a different approach, or whatever.

Write no more than half a page in total for this critique. In grading this, we will consider primarily how *helpful* your critique is likely to be the receiving team.

## Instructions
Go to your assigned project team's thread and reply to the thread with the designated artifact.
Please reply directly to that message. Don't nest replies. Don't create higher level threads.

## Submission
Submit a link to the critique thread in Commons.
