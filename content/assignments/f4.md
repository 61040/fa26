Your alpha checkpoint should be a complete MVP that compellingly demonstrates the viability of your idea, and it should be deployed.

## Deliverables
- **Source code**: the current state of your app, in your team repo.
- **Updated designs**: your designs from [F3](./f3.md), updated to reflect any changes.
- **Updated development plan**: a new version of the plan explaining what progress you've made, what remains to be done, and how your plans might have changed.
- **Deployed app**: a public URL pointing to your running app. See the [guide to deploying your app](../resources/deploying-apps.md).
- **Screen recording**: a video showing the current state of your app, with an audio narration explaining some key features. Link it in the README of your team repo.

## Submission
Submit your assignment in Commons. Only one team member needs to submit.
- The commit hash in your team project repo
- The URL of your deployed app
- The link to your screen recording
