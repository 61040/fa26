In your final submission, you have the opportunity to clean up your repository and meeting records, finalize your design documents and code, and provide two additional deliverables.

Deliverables:
- **Final version**: of project repository.
- **Design summary**: a succinct but thoughtful summary of the changes you made to your design since the original proposal in your functional design submission. Your mentoring meeting notes should help in reminding you of all the changes you made.
- **Reflection**: a reflection on your experiences in the final project, focusing on what you learned from the experience. Each team member should write their own reflection and place it in their personal repository.

Relevant rubrics: all, but especially
- [Reflective practice rubric](../rubrics/reflective-practice.md)
- [Technical writing rubric](../rubrics/technical-writing.md)
- [Professionalism rubric](../rubrics/professionalism.md)
