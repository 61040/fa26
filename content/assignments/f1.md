Each team will deliver a 3-4 minute presentation in person to the entire class. It is up to each team how to present, but we recommend that you prepare 3-5 slides and that you share the talking.

The content of the pitch is a dry run for your problem framing, and an opportunity to get feedback early on. It should include at least:

- **The bad situation**: that you've identified and why it matters
- **Some solution ideas**: an outline, or some ingredients
- **What you'd like help with**: risks, concerns, etc

The purpose of the project pitch is to encourage you to get going quickly on a project idea, and to get feedback with time to adjust your direction. It will also be graded, according to the rubric.

## Instructions
1. Create an initial team thread on Commons where the **title** is your **team name**. Feel free to include an intro :)
2. Reply to that team thread with a URL that is publically viewable of your pitch content.

See [here](https://class.mit-sdg.dev/t/f15b67e6-3b3b-4825-8227-a9d285dc7935) for an example of what your threads should look like.

## Submission
**Only one student need submit.**

1. Elect one member of your team to be the one who submits all deliverables for the duration of the team project.
2. That member should comment their team number on the [Final Project Logistics Thread](https://class.mit-sdg.dev/t/6fe0c1f7-6ea5-4fe9-8bf4-c7a42939d8e3) on Commons.
3. That member should submit the link to their public team thread (the one with
   the link to the pitch) on Commons.
