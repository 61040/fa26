Your user testing will follow the same protocol that you applied on your personal projects, as described [here](../assignments/p5.md), with the exception that a **recording is required**. You must have an audio (and video, if you'd like) recording for each user study.

You may choose to record the user study through [Zoom](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0062627) and save it to the cloud. This method will allow you to save an [audio transcript](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0064927&cms_guid=false&lang=en-US) which could be helpful for your report and analysis. Be sure to [enable sharing access](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0067567) to the recordings and upload the corresponding links to GitHub.

*The deliverables are*:

- **Task list**: instruction and rationale for each task.
- **Two recordings of the user studies**
- **Two study reports**: 200–300 words each.
- **Summary of lessons**: key design flaws or opportunities, with explanations, possible improvements, and classifications by level and severity.
