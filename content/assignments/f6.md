Your user testing will follow the same protocol that you applied on your personal projects, as described [here](../assignments/p5.md), with two exceptions.
- A **recording is required**. You must have an audio (and video, if you'd like) recording for each user study. You may choose to record the user study through [Zoom](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0062627) and save it to the cloud. This method will allow you to save an [audio transcript](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0064927&cms_guid=false&lang=en-US) which could be helpful for your report and analysis. Be sure to [enable sharing access](https://support.zoom.com/hc/en/article?id=zm_kb&sysparm_article=KB0067567) to the recordings and save the corresponding links for submission.
- You will make one improvement to your app that you found from the user study, but this will be due with F7, *not* F6. F7 is due two days after F6, so we recommend that you start on your improvement before you submit F6.

## Deliverables

- **Task list**: instruction and rationale for each task.
- **Two recordings of the user studies**
- **Two study reports**: 200–300 words each.
- **Summary of lessons**: key design flaws or opportunities, with explanations, possible improvements, and classifications by level and severity.

Keep the task list, study reports, and summary of lessons in your team repo, linked from the README. For your recordings, to preserve user privacy, **submit the links to the recordings directly in the Commons submission box** and **make sure course staff can open them without requesting access**.

## Submission
Submit your assignment in Commons.
- Full link to the specific commit of your project repo
- Links to your user study recordings
