title: "Final Project: Ethical Analysis"
---

**Purpose.** Practice using value-sensitive design to examine the wider consequences of your design choices. Include this analysis in your [F2 problem framing submission](./f2.md).

## Tasks

Use the four dimensions introduced in the [VSD lecture](/lectures/vsd.pdf):

- **Stakeholders:** Who is affected, including people who never use the app?
- **Time:** How might effects change as people adapt to the technology, repurpose it, or stop using it?
- **Pervasiveness:** What might change if the technology becomes widely adopted?
- **Values:** Which values does the design support or threaten, and where might those values conflict?

Review the prompts for these four dimensions in the [Envisioning Cards toolkit](https://vsdesign.org/toolkits/envisioningcards/). The toolkit also includes Multi-lifespan cards; those are not required for this assignment.

Consider each dimension in relation to your problem framing and proposed features. Look for opportunities to create benefits as well as possible harms.

Write at least **five insights, each 30-75 words**, containing:

1. **Observation:** Identify the dimension, prompt, and feature involved, and explain the consequence you foresee.
2. **Design response:** Explain how you would respond by removing, limiting, adjusting, extending, or adding a feature.

## Advice

Consider benefits beyond those experienced by individual users. As the lecture's curb-cut example illustrates, a change addressing one group's needs can benefit others too.

Research your domain to inform the analysis. Relevant reporting and examples from existing technologies can help you identify consequences that brainstorming alone might miss.

## Rubric

Your analysis will be assessed using the [value-sensitive design rubric](../rubrics/value-sensitive-design.md).
